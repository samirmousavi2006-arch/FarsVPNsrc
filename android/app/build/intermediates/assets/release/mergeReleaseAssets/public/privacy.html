Privacy Policy for FarsVPN

Effective Date: September 1, 2026

FarsVPN ("we", "our", or "us") operates the FarsVPN mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

A. Personal Information: If you sign up or log in using Google Sign-In or Email, we collect your email address and basic profile information provided by Firebase Authentication solely for user identity management.

B. Guest Mode: Users can use FarsVPN as a Guest without providing any personal information or email addresses.

C. VPN Connection Logs: We maintain a strict zero-content-logging policy. We do NOT inspect, log, or store your browsing history, DNS queries, traffic destination, or data content routed through our VPN servers.

D. Operational Connection Metadata: To manage active sessions and prevent service abuse, we temporarily record session duration and active node IDs in Firebase Firestore while a connection is active.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and operate our Reverse VPN service.
  • To manage user authentication and active 2-hour session limits.
  • To serve third-party advertisements via Google AdMob for Guest users.

3. Third-Party Services

FarsVPN integrates the following third-party services which may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Firebase Authentication & Firestore
  • Google AdMob

4. Data Safety & Security

We use industry-standard WireGuard encryption protocols to secure all data transmitted through our VPN tunnels. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, contact us at: support@farsvpn.local
